Try it in five minutes

Follow these steps in order; each one shows a different person’s job.

  1. Sign in as ScottOpen the prototype, choose Scott, select Continue.
  2. Move a dealPipeline → change Finance transformation team to Opportunity. A follow-up task appears.
  3. Open an accountCompanies → Northstar Health. Read the AI summary, then log a note in the timeline.
  4. Work the queueMy tasks → Work my queue → Start. Pick a template and send.
  5. Switch to RyanSign out, choose Ryan, open Events. Select Preview this week’s email.
  6. Switch to DannyReports for the leadership dashboards, then Events → Budget & ROI.
  7. Back to Scott as adminSettings → Admin → Onboard user. The new person appears on the sign-in screen.

Cadences

Scott

Short sequences of 3–5 emails that stop the moment someone replies, bounces or unsubscribes.

What it shows

  • Round table alumni invite, AI readiness follow-up, post-event thank-you
  • Steps with business-day gaps; reply rate, bounces, unsubscribes

Try this

  2. Select Unsubscribed: they join the suppression list

Prototype vs production build

The screens carry over to production unchanged; what changes is that the data becomes shared and the connections become real.

AreaIn the prototypeIn production
Sign-inPick a personMicrosoft 365 single sign-on (Entra ID); admins add people to a StaffCRM group
Data storageSaved in your own browser onlyAzure Database for PostgreSQL shared by the whole team, with nightly backups
Email and meetingsSample timeline entriesOutlook mail and calendar sync through Microsoft Graph for known contacts
Sending emailLogged, not sent; drafts can open in OutlookSent from each person’s own mailbox; replies and bounces stop cadences automatically
ZoomInfoSample company returnedZoomInfo API (search free, enrich on save) or CSV if the license has no API
LinkedInURL captureURL capture plus Janium exports; no LinkedIn profile API is available
AI account summaryAssembled from the timelineAzure OpenAI in the firm’s own tenant; nothing sent without approval
Reminder and digest emailsPreviews onlyScheduled emails on Mondays and the 1st of the month
HubSpot and Janium importsPreviews and sample rowsReal imports with matching on email, LinkedIn URL and company domain

Plan and cost

Phase 1 is up from the original 700–850 hours because the event calendar, event budget and admin controls joined the scope. Run cost is unchanged: about $2.5–5K a year for Azure hosting plus $0.3–1K for AI, against about $15K a year for HubSpot. These are estimates to confirm with the Azure pricing calculator. Decisions needed

Most of these can be settled in the demo itself.

  • Approve the production build, and decide who builds it: the firm’s developers, Errol, or both.
  • Confirm the HubSpot renewal date, which sets the cutover deadline.
  • Pick the stage names: Scott’s (Lead, MQL, SQL, Opportunity) or Monika’s (Lead, Account, Meeting, Deal); keep or drop “SQL Potential”.
  • Confirm who gets leadership access beyond the CEO, CSO and COO, and whether Marketing should see the Pipeline.
  • Agree who approves event spend in Budget & ROI, and any dollar threshold.
  • Confirm Stephen’s event list can be exported as CSV in the template’s columns.
  • Confirm the ZoomInfo license includes API access.
